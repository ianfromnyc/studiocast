BiRefNet Lite ONNX Model Pack for StudioCast (Open CUDA backend)

Upstream model: onnx-community/BiRefNet_lite-ONNX
License shown by upstream: MIT

This pack downloads the ONNX weights from Hugging Face and patches the model
graph to expose a sigmoid(alpha) output named "alpha" for runtime compatibility.

You must review and comply with the upstream license and any dataset/model terms:
- Hugging Face model page: onnx-community/BiRefNet_lite-ONNX
